Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Downexa is an independent service and is not affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

What we collect

Downexa does not require an account, and we never ask for your Instagram username or password. When you press Download Now, the link you pasted is sent to our server so it can be processed. We do not build a history of the links you submit.

How your link is used

  • Our server forwards the link to a third-party media processing provider to look up the public media.
  • Our hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard technical logs, such as IP address, time, and request details, for security and reliability.
  • We use your IP address only to limit abuse (rate limiting).

On your device

Your browser stores your light or dark theme choice in local storage. Nothing else is stored, and no advertising or tracking cookies are used by this site.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address. Media files you download come directly from the platform that hosts them, under that platform's own terms and privacy policy.

Your choices

You can clear your browser storage at any time. To ask about data we may hold, contact us using the email below.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
